Example Manchester Workshop: website rebuild brief

Use this as a starting brief, then confirm scope and price with your supplier.

Why we are rebuilding
- Difficult to use on mobile
- A worthwhile result: A simple mobile enquiry form and clearer project examples.
- Current website: https://example.co.uk/

Keep working during the change
- Contact forms
- Downloads

Information to preserve
- Commercial services
- Project photographs
- Existing customer help

Before work begins
- Arrange owner access to the domain, website, hosting and relevant business accounts before starting.
- Ask the supplier to preserve business email records and test email after any domain or hosting change.
- Agree deliverables, ownership, ongoing costs and what is included in the handover.

Launch checks
- Have the supplier map important old page addresses to appropriate new pages.
- Test enquiries, booking links, mobile navigation and downloads before and after launch.
- Agree a backup and a route to resolve launch problems.
